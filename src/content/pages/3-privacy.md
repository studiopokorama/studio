## 1. What information we collect

We currently do **not** offer player accounts in our game, and we do **not** ask users to register.

We may collect the following types of information:

### Information collected automatically

When you use our Services, certain information may be collected automatically through our tools and service providers, including Firebase. This may include:

- basic device and app information;
- approximate location derived from IP address or device settings;
- browser or operating system information;
- language settings;
- usage data, such as interactions with the website or game;
- app events and session information;
- technical information related to notifications;
- cookies and similar technologies on the website.

Because we use **Firebase Analytics**, the exact categories of technical and usage data may depend on how Firebase operates on a given platform and version of our Services.

### Information you provide directly

If you contact us by email at [studio@pokorama.com](mailto:studio@pokorama.com), we collect the information you choose to include in your message, such as:

- your email address;
- your name, if you provide it;
- the contents of your message;
- any attachments or other information you send us.

### Local gameplay data

Game progress and similar gameplay data are currently stored **locally on your device or browser**, not in a Studio Pokorama account.

If you use a **Reset Game** feature, local progress or settings may be erased or restarted.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to:

- operate and provide the Services;
- understand how users interact with the Services;
- improve gameplay, website performance, and user experience;
- send push notifications where enabled;
- maintain security and prevent misuse;
- respond to support or business inquiries sent by email;
- comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

## 3. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website uses **cookies and similar technologies**. These may be used to:

- support basic website functionality;
- understand website usage and performance;
- support analytics and related technical operations.

You can usually control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect how the website works.

## 4. Push notifications

We use **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)** to send push notifications where supported and where you have allowed notifications on your device or browser.

You can usually control notifications through your device, browser, or platform settings.

## 5. Third-party services

We use third-party service providers to help operate the Services.

### Firebase

We use **Firebase**, including:

- **Firebase Analytics**
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)**

These tools may process technical and usage data in order to provide analytics, messaging, and related service functionality.

We may also rely on platform providers such as app stores, browser platforms, Steam, or itch.io when distributing our Services. Their own privacy practices apply to their platforms and services.

## 6. Legal basis for processing

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with similar rules, we process personal data only where we have a valid legal basis, including:

- our **legitimate interests** in operating, securing, improving, and understanding the Services;
- your **consent**, where required, for example for certain cookies or notifications;
- compliance with a **legal obligation**;
- taking steps connected with a request you make to us, such as contacting support.

We do **not** sell your personal data.

We may share information:

- where necessary to protect our rights, users, or Services;

We share information only as reasonably necessary for these purposes.

## 8. International data transfers

Some of our service providers may process data outside your country of residence.

Where required by law, we take appropriate steps to protect personal data transferred internationally.

## 9. Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including to:

- respond to inquiries;
- maintain security and records;

How long data is kept may depend on the type of data, how it is used, and legal or operational requirements.

We do **not knowingly collect personal data from children** except as necessary to operate the Services. Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal data may contact us to request review or deletion where applicable.
